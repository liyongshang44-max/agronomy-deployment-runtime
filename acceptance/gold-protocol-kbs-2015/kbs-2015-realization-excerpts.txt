KBS 2015 Agronomic Protocol — action realization excerpts
Page 20: Plots are tilled as needed (2-6 times a year) to prevent plant growth from becoming established.
Page 20: Tillage can be soil finishing, rototilling or any tillage that keeps plant growth from becoming established.
Page 20: Plots can be chisel plowed and soil finished if more aggressive tillage is needed.
